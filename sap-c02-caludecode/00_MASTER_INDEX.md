## Critical Comparisons to Memorize

These appear on EVERY exam:

1. **Security Groups vs NACLs** (stateful vs stateless)
2. **ALB vs NLB** (Layer 7 vs Layer 4)
3. **Multi-AZ vs Read Replica** (HA vs performance)
4. **RDS vs DynamoDB** (relational vs NoSQL)
5. **Aurora vs RDS** (cloud-native vs standard)
6. **GSI vs LSI** (different PK vs same PK, creation time)
7. **SQS vs SNS** (queue vs pub/sub)
8. **Kinesis vs SQS** (streaming vs messaging)
9. **SSE-S3 vs SSE-KMS vs SSE-C** (encryption)
10. **Gateway Endpoint vs Interface Endpoint** (S3/DDB free vs other services paid)
11. **VPC Peering vs Transit Gateway** (non-transitive vs hub)
12. **Direct Connect vs VPN** (dedicated vs internet)
13. **CloudFront vs Global Accelerator** (caching vs routing)
14. **EFS vs FSx vs EBS vs S3** (file vs block vs object)
15. **Lambda vs EC2 vs Fargate** (serverless vs managed vs container)
